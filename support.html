Peek-a-Pop

Support and answers to common questions.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?

Email inatasunsoft@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model, the level number and what happened. A screenshot helps most.

How to play

Tap a fruit block to turn its gaze. When two matching fruits look right at each other, they pop. Pops can chain. Clear the tray before your taps run out. No timer, and the game plays offline.

Common questions

Is every level solvable?

Yes. Every level is checked by the game's own solver before you see it. If you get stuck, use Undo, a Hint, or restart the level.

What do the special fruits do?

Stones block the view. Sleepy fruit wake up when a neighbour pops. Frozen fruit can't turn until two pops next to them crack the ice. Rainbow fruit pop with any fruit. Dizzy fruit turn by themselves every time you tap. Shy fruit turn the other way.

How do I keep my progress on a new phone?

Tap Sign in on the title screen to save with Google Play Games, then sign in with the same account on the new phone.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads.

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account you bought with.
