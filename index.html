Privacy Policy

Peek-a-Pop · Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you play Peek-a-Pop ("the game") by Inata Sun Soft, published on Google Play (Android) and the Apple App Store (iOS) under the identifier com.inatasunsoft.peekapop.

The short version

The game has no accounts of its own and no server of its own. It never asks for your name, phone number or location, and we never receive your name, email, phone number or precise location. A small amount of data is handled by Google (ads, purchases, Play Games) and by RevenueCat (purchase records), as described below.

Information the game stores on your device

  • the highest level you have reached and your stars for each level
  • your coin balance and whether you bought Remove Ads
  • whether sound is on, which tips you have already seen, and the date you last claimed the daily gift
  • identifiers of purchases already credited, so a purchase is never counted twice

This stays in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play Games sign-in and cloud save (optional, Android only)

If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, the game saves a copy of the progress listed above to your own Google account ("Saved Games"), so it can be restored on a new phone. Google provides your Play Games player profile to the game for this; we do not receive your email address or contacts. You can stop at any time by signing out of Play Games, and you can delete the saved copy from your Google account's Play Games settings.

Purchases

On Android, purchases are handled by Google Play Billing; on iOS, by Apple's App Store. RevenueCat confirms them on both.

Purchases (Remove Ads and coin packs) are handled by Google Play Billing. The game uses RevenueCat to confirm purchases: RevenueCat receives the purchase record (product, time, price, a purchase token) linked to a random anonymous ID created by the app. We never see your payment details. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how it is played and to fix problems. It records events such as starting or finishing a level, finishing a Rush or Daily Puzzle, completing the tutorial, watching or failing to load a rewarded ad, and when the rating prompt is shown, together with basic device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version) and a random app-instance ID. These events contain no name, email or other personal data and are not linked to your identity. On iOS, the analytics SDK is used without access to the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). Data is processed by Google under its privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob (rewarded videos you choose to watch, and occasional ads between levels). Players who buy Remove Ads see no ads. To show ads, AdMob collects:

WhatWhy
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier on your phone)To select ads and limit repeats
Device information, such as model, OS version and languageTo serve an ad that fits your device
Approximate location, derived from your IP addressTo serve region-appropriate ads and meet local laws
Interactions with ads, such as views and tapsTo measure advertising and pay the developer

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change that choice later from the Privacy link on the title screen. This data is handled by Google under policies.google.com/privacy; see also how Google uses data from apps.

On iPhone and iPad, the game asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before AdMob may use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). If you tap Ask App Not to Track, AdMob does not use the IDFA and shows non-personalized ads. You can change this later in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the game also asks for consent to personalized ads.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads lets you reset or delete it and turn off ad personalisation.
  • iOS: Settings, Privacy & Security, Tracking (turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or turn it off for this app), and Settings, Privacy & Security, Apple Advertising to limit ad personalization. Android: Settings, Google, Ads.
  • Cloud save: sign out of Play Games, and delete the saved game from your Google account.
  • Everything on the device: uninstall the game.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Security

Data sent to Google and RevenueCat travels over encrypted connections. The game keeps no server of its own, so there is no account of yours on our side to lose.
